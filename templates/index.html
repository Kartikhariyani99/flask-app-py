02. About Me

Building a path into DevOps & Cloud

I'm Kartik Hariyani, a Computer Engineering student at Vishwakarma Government Engineering College, Ahmedabad, currently in my 7th semester. My interest in infrastructure and automation grew out of building full-stack and academic projects, which pushed me to look beyond writing code and into how applications are actually packaged, deployed, and kept running.

That curiosity led me toward DevOps and Cloud Engineering — learning Docker to containerize applications, experimenting with Kubernetes clusters for orchestration, and getting comfortable working in Linux environments. I'm steadily building my understanding of CI/CD pipelines and cloud platforms like AWS through hands-on projects rather than theory alone.

Alongside my coursework in core Computer Science subjects, I enjoy translating what I learn into small, practical labs — spinning up clusters, writing Dockerfiles, and documenting the process as I go.
