Who we are

Born on the Edge of Cold

FrostBurst was founded in 2018 by two beverage scientists obsessed with pushing refreshment to its absolute limit. Every can is brewed in small batches with natural flavors and purified water.

We don't do lukewarm. In taste, in quality, or in values. Our drinks are free from artificial preservatives, and every bottle is packaged in 100% recyclable aluminium.
